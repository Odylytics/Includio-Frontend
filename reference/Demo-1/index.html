B2B inclusive hiring platform

Inclusive hiring starts with an interview everyone can actually do.

Includio pairs a structured, AI-assisted interview builder for HR with a private practice studio for candidates — so a different way of communicating is never mistaken for a lack of ability.

Demo only — AI, recording and calendar features are simulated.

The product, end to end

Five connected steps — not a black-box AI screen.

  1. 01Employer configures

    Job description, competencies, practice quota and interview settings.

  2. 02AI proposes a plan

    Questions, competency coverage and timing — all editable by HR.

  3. 03Candidate practices

    A private studio inside a structured, employer-set window.

  4. 04Candidate interviews

    The approved format, with agreed accommodations applied.

  5. 05HR reviews evidence

    Transcript, timeline and competency excerpts for a human decision.

What Includio refuses to do

  • No behavioural scoring from eye contact, tone or speech speed.
  • No practice history shared with HR unless the candidate shares it.
  • No AI hiring decision, and no automatic candidate ranking.
  • No penalty for needing more time to think.

Candidate
